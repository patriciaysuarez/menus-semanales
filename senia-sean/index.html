      Reheating.

      Soups
      • Stove: warm over low heat, stirring now and then, until steaming (about 4–8 minutes).
      • Microwave: loosely covered, in 1-minute rounds, stirring between each.
      • If it thickened in the fridge, add a splash of water or stock.
      Grains
      • Add a spoonful of water, cover, and microwave 1–2 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
      • Or warm in a skillet over low heat with a little water or oil, covered.
      Green salads
      • Serve cold. Do not heat.
      • Keep dressing separate and toss right before eating so the leaves stay crisp.
      Vegetables
      • Oven or toaster oven: 375–400°F for 8–12 minutes for crisp edges.
      • Microwave: 45–90 seconds, softer texture.
      Meat, poultry & fish
      • Oven: covered, 325–350°F for 10–15 minutes with a splash of sauce or broth to keep it moist.
      • Microwave: medium power in short rounds so it does not dry out.
      • Fish: go gentle and short. Warm just until it is no longer cold in the center.
      • Warm sauces separately on the stove and pour over.
      Pasta
      • Skillet: medium-low heat with a splash of water or sauce, tossing until hot.
      • Microwave: covered, with a spoonful of water, 1–2 minutes, stirring halfway.
      • Baked pasta: covered with foil at 350°F for 15–20 minutes.
      Desserts
      • Most are best at room temperature or chilled.
      • Baked goods: 5–8 minutes at 300°F to bring back the softness.
