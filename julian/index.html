      Reheating.

      Soups

      Stove: Warm over low heat, stirring occasionally, until hot (about 4–8 minutes).

      If it thickened, add a splash of water or stock.

      Grains

      Cover with a damp paper towel and microwave 1–2 minutes. Fluff with a fork. Or warm in a skillet, covered, over low heat with a splash of water or oil.

      Green salads

      Serve cold. Shake dressing before serving.

      Grain & Noodle Salads

      Eat at room temperature or slightly heated through.

      Vegetables

      Oven or air fryer: 375–400°F for 8–12 minutes for crisp edges.

      Meat, Poultry & Fish

      Oven: Heat covered at 325–350°F for 10–15 minutes.

      Microwave: Heat in short intervals to avoid drying.

      Fish: Heat in 30 second intervals, or just until warmed through. Warm sauces separately and pour over.

      Pasta

      Skillet: Heat on medium-low with a splash of water or sauce, stirring until hot.

      Microwave: Heat covered, with a spoonful of water for 1–2 minutes, stirring halfway.

      Baked pasta: Heat covered with foil at 350°F for 15–20 minutes.
